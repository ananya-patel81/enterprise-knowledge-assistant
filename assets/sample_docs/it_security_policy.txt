Acme Corp — IT Security Policy (Excerpt)

1. PASSWORD POLICY

All employees must use passwords that are at least 12 characters long
and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and
symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Reuse of the last 5
passwords is not permitted.

To reset a forgotten password, employees should visit the Identity
Portal at id.acmecorp.internal and select "Forgot Password". A
verification code will be sent to the employee's registered mobile
number. If the employee no longer has access to that number, they must
contact the IT Helpdesk in person with their employee ID badge.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for all systems that store customer data, including
the CRM, the billing system, and all production databases. Employees
must enroll in MFA using the Acme Authenticator app within their first
3 days of joining.

3. DEVICE SECURITY

All company laptops must have disk encryption enabled (BitLocker on
Windows, FileVault on macOS). Lost or stolen devices must be reported
to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery, by emailing
security@acmecorp.com or calling the 24/7 security hotline.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

Data is classified into four tiers:
- Public: marketing materials, public documentation.
- Internal: meeting notes, internal wikis, non-sensitive emails.
- Confidential: source code, financial reports, HR records.
- Restricted: customer PII, payment information, security credentials.

Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems (the production
database and the encrypted secrets vault). Restricted data must never
be copied to personal devices, USB drives, or third-party cloud storage
such as personal Google Drive or Dropbox accounts.

5. INCIDENT RESPONSE

In the event of a suspected security breach, employees must immediately
notify the Security team and avoid discussing the incident on public
channels (including company-wide Slack channels) until the Security
team has issued guidance. The Security team will follow the documented
Incident Response Runbook, which includes containment, eradication,
recovery, and a post-incident review within 5 business days.

6. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS

Vendors and contractors requiring access to internal systems must be
sponsored by an internal employee and undergo a security review by the
Security team before provisioning any credentials. Vendor access is
automatically revoked 90 days after the contract end date unless
explicitly renewed.
