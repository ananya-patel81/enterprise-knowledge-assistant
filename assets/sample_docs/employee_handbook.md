# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, credited at the rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service.
Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next
calendar year; any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31st.

Sick leave is granted separately, with 10 days per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager
approval. Engineering and Data teams may request fully remote
arrangements for up to 3 months per year, subject to written approval
from their department head.

## Expense Reimbursement

All business expenses must be submitted through the Expense Portal
within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses submitted after 30 days
will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by
Finance.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Laptop and Equipment Policy

New employees are issued a company laptop within their first week. The
standard laptop refresh cycle is 3 years. Employees relocating
internationally must return company equipment to the local IT team
before departure.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary
information, including source code, customer data, and unreleased
product plans. Violations of confidentiality may result in termination
and legal action.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October.
Reviews include a self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer
feedback from at least 2 colleagues. Ratings range from "Needs
Improvement" to "Exceptional", and directly influence the annual
compensation review cycle that follows in November.
